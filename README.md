Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-the-houseplant-out-of-office-7c586047): the funniest out-of-office auto-reply a houseplant would send, in 120 characters or fewer.

## The entry

> Out of office. I'm a fern, unwatered since March. Your email has been composted. I needed the nutrients.

104 characters. A neglected fern's auto-reply: the neglect is the setup, the email turned to compost is the surprise, and the plant eating it for nutrients is the second beat. Plant life drives the joke — no office cliche, no pun.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
